# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work.
Harassment or discriminatory behaviour is not tolerated. Report concerns privately to the maintainers
(use the contact on the repository profile); reports are handled confidentially.
